## 答案解析

答案：**1 B　2 D　3 A　4 C　5 A　6 D　7 B　8 C　9 A**。

### R01-L1-Q1（题1）｜B

考点：根据前后行动作有限推断。第1段先“watched through the open door”，提供材料后“came in and began to draw”，支持材料帮助这两人加入；不能断言他们家里一定没有材料。

A：旁观不等于不喜欢。C：没有邀请全校的信息。D：没有原六人停止画画的信息。

### R01-L1-Q2（题2）｜D

考点：由物品去向推断用途。第2段画带回家，铅笔全部放回盒子，盒子留在教室，支持铅笔供在这里使用；不能推出具体所有者或价格。

A：与放回铅笔冲突。B：不只Sam使用。C：没有购买要求。

### R01-L1-Q3（题3）｜A

考点：从行动和便条推断愿望。第2段Sam周四主动返回，问能否“on another day too”，支持想增加画画机会。

B：“too”支持增加而非取消。C：没有周二不能来的信息，“never”过度。D：没有带走铅笔的请求。

### R01-L2-Q1（题4）｜C

考点：结合数字与行为判断贡献。第1段Wei浇水数是零，却教低年级学生放种子；学生随后能独立正确完成。零浇水量不等于没有贡献。

A：与“without his help”冲突。B：无不愿劳动的依据。D：与零浇水量冲突。

### R01-L2-Q2（题5）｜A

考点：结合天气与检查行为推断原因。第2段周四下了一夜雨，Anna周五先检查每株植物再取水。雨后许多植物已有水，是现有线索支持的最佳解释；“probably”保留推断边界。

B：与检查每株植物冲突。C：没有植物被拿走的信息。D：没有离组计划。

### R01-L2-Q3（题6）｜D

考点：由试行步骤推断决策态度。第3段先“try this for four weeks”，之后讨论并决定下一步，说明先用实际经验再作进一步决定。

A：只确定四周试行，不是全年。B：尚未预先知道效果。C：记录其他工作不等于取消浇水。

### R01-L3-Q1（题7）｜B

考点：区分总量、人数与个体分配。第1段两阶段均是“the same twelve students”，总借阅量24变为36；只能确认同一群体借书总量上升。

A：没有新增借阅者。C：36÷12＝3只是平均值，不能推出人人借3本。D：20是新书数量，不是新书借出数量。

### R01-L3-Q2（题8）｜C

考点：多条件同时变化时限制因果结论。第1段同时增加开放时间和新书；第2段8人认为晚开有用，其中3人也选新书。两项改变都可能有作用，现有资料不能拆分各自贡献。“may”不等于证明两者都有独立效果。

A：不能排除开放时间的作用。B：不能排除新书的作用。D：没有贡献恰好相等的证据。

### R01-L3-Q3（题9）｜A

考点：结合已调查者和补充对象推断目的。第2段只问了12名借阅者；第3段还要询问两个阶段均未使用图书馆的人，可了解仍阻碍他们使用的原因；不预设原因一定是开放时间。

B：这些人并未借书。C：已有2名借阅者不支持晚开，不能证明全员赞成。D：两个阶段都未使用，不能说第二阶段借得更少。

## 完整题面

# 八年级阅读分层训练｜学生卷

姓名：__________　日期：__________

阅读三篇短文，选择每题的最佳答案。共3篇、9题，每题只有一个最佳答案。

## L1 基础｜The Art Room

Every Tuesday, six children drew in Room 3. Two other children often watched through the open door. One week, the teacher put pencils and paper on a table by the door. The two children came in and began to draw.

At the end, the children took their pictures home and put all the pencils back in the box. The teacher left the box in Room 3. On Thursday, Sam returned alone to draw, but the door was closed. He left a note: “Could we use the room on another day too?”

**1. What does the first paragraph suggest?**

A. The two children did not like drawing before.
B. Having pencils and paper there helped the two children join.
C. The teacher asked every child in the school to come.
D. The six children stopped drawing that week.

**2. What can we learn about the pencils?**

A. Each child took a new pencil home.
B. The teacher gave the pencils to Sam alone.
C. The children needed to buy them after drawing.
D. They were there for children to use in the room.

**3. What does Sam’s note suggest?**

A. He wanted more chances to draw in the room.
B. He wanted the Tuesday group to stop meeting.
C. He could never come on Tuesdays.
D. He wanted to take the box of pencils home.

## L2 进阶｜Work in the Garden

The school garden group wrote down how many plants each student watered. Last week, Wei’s number was zero. He spent his time showing younger students where to put seeds. Later, they put the seeds in the right places without his help.

Anna watered ten plants on Monday but only two on Friday. It rained all Thursday night. On Friday, she checked every plant before she carried any water to the garden.

Next month, the group will also write down other jobs, such as helping new students. The teacher said, “Let’s try this for four weeks. Then we can talk about it and decide what to do next.”

**4. What can we learn about Wei’s work last week?**

A. It made the younger students need help every time.
B. It showed that he did not want to work in the garden.
C. It helped the group, although his watering number was zero.
D. It made him the student who watered the most plants.

**5. Why did Anna probably water fewer plants on Friday?**

A. The rain had already given many plants water.
B. She stopped checking the plants that day.
C. The younger students had taken most of the plants away.
D. She had decided to leave the group.

**6. What does the teacher’s plan suggest?**

A. The group must use the new way for the whole year.
B. The teacher already knows that the new way will work well.
C. Students will no longer do any watering.
D. The group will use its experience before making a further decision.

## L3 挑战｜A Library Change

For two weeks, the school library opened only at lunchtime. Twelve students borrowed 24 books. During the next two weeks, the library opened at lunchtime and after school. It also put 20 new storybooks on a table near the door. The same twelve students borrowed 36 books.

Eight of the twelve came after school and said the later opening time was useful. Three of these eight also chose books from the new table. The teacher asked all twelve students whether the library should keep opening after school. Ten said yes; two said no.

Before making a decision, the teacher also wanted to talk to students who had not used the library during either two-week period.

**7. What do the borrowing numbers show?**

A. More students began to borrow books.
B. The same group borrowed more books in the second period.
C. Each of the twelve students borrowed three books in the second period.
D. All the new storybooks were borrowed.

**8. Which idea is best supported by the passage?**

A. The new storybooks alone made students borrow more books.
B. The later opening time alone made students borrow more books.
C. Both changes may have helped; the passage does not show how much each helped.
D. The two changes helped the borrowing numbers rise by exactly the same amount.

**9. Why did the teacher probably want to talk to students who had not used the library?**

A. To find out whether something was still stopping them from using it.
B. To ask them which new books they had borrowed.
C. To show that all students wanted the library to open later.
D. To find out why they had borrowed fewer books in the second period.
